Delivery Policy

Effective date: 01 September 2026 Last updated: 01 September 2026

This Delivery Policy explains how TevrocSoft manages project delivery, milestones, schedules, acceptance procedures, and responsibilities for digital products, software work, website builds, and professional services delivered under our agreements.

1. Scope of Delivery

TevrocSoft delivers services and digital outputs in accordance with the agreed scope, project brief, proposal, statement of work, or signed engagement document. Delivery may include strategy, design, development, configuration, implementation, testing, training, support, and relevant project documentation.

The final deliverable is deemed to be the items expressly listed in the project agreement, along with any documented addenda or approved revisions. Any additional requests outside the agreed scope may be priced separately as change requests.

2. Project Timeline and Milestones

Delivery timelines are based on the agreed milestone schedule and may be dependent on the client’s responsiveness, timely approvals, access to systems, and the availability of required content or assets.

TevrocSoft will provide reasonable estimates for milestones, but timelines are not guaranteed where there are delays caused by incomplete information, late feedback, third-party dependencies, or external operational circumstances.

3. Client Responsibilities

To ensure successful delivery, the client must provide the information, access, approvals, content, and infrastructure required for the project to proceed as planned. This includes but is not limited to business requirements, branding assets, content, domain information, credentials, and stakeholder sign-off.

  • Provide complete and timely project briefings and approval feedback.
  • Grant access to required platforms, accounts, services, or third-party systems.
  • Review deliverables within the agreed review window.
  • Ensure that all required legal, technical, and operational prerequisites are in place.

4. Acceptance and Sign-Off

TevrocSoft will present outputs for review at each agreed milestone or completion stage. Acceptance is considered valid when the client confirms approval in writing, or when the client fails to provide feedback within the agreed review period, unless otherwise stated in the engagement agreement.

Where requested changes are submitted after approval, those modifications may be classified as additional scope and may require revised timelines and additional costs.

5. Delivery Channels and Deployment

Deliverables may be provided electronically, through a staging environment, via a managed hosting platform, or by other agreed delivery channels. For projects involving deployment, launch, or integration, client approval and platform access are required before final activation or go-live.

TevrocSoft is not responsible for issues arising from third-party hosting providers, domain registrars, external APIs, or infrastructure changes outside the direct control of the project team.

6. Change Requests and Revisions

Any change requests beyond the agreed scope, including additional pages, features, integrations, redesign work, or extended support, may be treated as additional work and will be subject to revised timelines and pricing.

TevrocSoft will communicate the effect of such changes before proceeding, and the client must confirm the amended scope before work continues.

7. Delays and Force Majeure

TevrocSoft will make reasonable efforts to meet agreed delivery dates, but may not be liable for delays caused by factors outside its reasonable control. This includes but is not limited to shortages, third-party service outages, internet instability, client-side delays, regulatory restrictions, and force majeure events.

Where delays occur for reasons attributable to the client or to external dependencies, the project timeline may be extended, and TevrocSoft reserves the right to revise delivery dates accordingly.

8. Support and Maintenance

Support, maintenance, hosting, and after-launch assistance may be delivered under separate support terms or a support agreement. Where no support agreement exists, TevrocSoft may provide limited support only as expressly agreed in writing.

Any post-delivery support requested beyond the agreed scope will be handled as a separate support or service request and may be billed according to the applicable rate card.

9. Ownership and Transfer

Upon full payment and completion of the agreed work, the client will generally obtain ownership or rights to the delivered output as expressly set out in the contract. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, TevrocSoft retains ownership of pre-existing frameworks, reusable code, templates, tooling, and methodologies.

When deliverables are transferred, the client is responsible for continuing support, hosting, operating, and securing the final product in line with the agreed deployment configuration and any supporting instructions provided by TevrocSoft.

10. Contact and Notices

If you have questions about delivery timelines, acceptance, scope, or project handover, please contact TevrocSoft at info@tevrocsoft.co.za. Written communications may also be sent to the registered business address associated with TevrocSoft from time to time.
