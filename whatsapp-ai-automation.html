WhatsApp AI questions, answered

How do you prevent made-up answers?

No AI setup can guarantee a perfect answer every time. A responsible design limits the information used, gives the system a way to indicate uncertainty, and provides an escalation path when a response is not supported.

Can it update other business systems?

That depends on the available integrations, permissions, and the action being considered. We review those dependencies and agree which actions need validation or human approval before they are automated.
