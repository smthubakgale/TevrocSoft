1. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject, you may ask TevrocSoft to explain how we process your personal information and to help you exercise the rights available under POPIA.

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal information.
  • Request access to, or a description of, your personal information.
  • Request correction, updating, or deletion of inaccurate or unlawfully held information.
  • Object to processing where POPIA permits an objection.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to direct marketing communications and automated decision-making where applicable.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator if you believe your information has been processed unlawfully.

2. How to submit a request

Send your request to our Information Officer at info@tevrocsoft.co.za. Please include:

  • Your full name and preferred contact details.
  • A clear description of the right you want to exercise.
  • Enough information for us to locate the relevant record or account.
  • A copy of identification or other information reasonably needed to verify your identity.

3. Verification and response

We may ask for additional information to verify your identity and protect personal information from unauthorised disclosure. We will assess and respond to a valid request within the time required by applicable law.

If we cannot comply with a request, we will explain the reason, subject to lawful limitations. We may charge a lawful fee or require a prescribed form where POPIA permits this.

4. Requests to correct or delete information

Tell us which information is inaccurate, incomplete, excessive, outdated, or unlawfully processed and what correction you request. We will take reasonable steps to update, delete, or stop using information where required by POPIA, while retaining information that we must keep for legal, contractual, security, or legitimate business purposes.

5. Direct marketing

You may opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe option in the message or by contacting info@tevrocsoft.co.za. We will still send essential service, account, or legal notices where necessary.

6. Complaints

Please contact us first so that we can investigate and resolve your concern. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa:
