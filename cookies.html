This Cookie Policy explains how TevrocSoft uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and digital platforms. By continuing to browse our website, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this document.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

Cookies do different jobs, including helping pages load correctly, remembering your preferences, and improving the overall user experience. They may also help ensure that online content and advertisements are relevant to your interests.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes, including functionality, analytics, security, and preference management. In most cases, there are no standard ways to disable cookies without affecting the website experience and core features.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot usually be turned off in our systems. They are generally set in response to actions such as logging in, selecting preferences, or completing forms.

You may set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some site features may then not work as intended. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information.

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us measure visits and traffic sources so we can improve performance. They tell us which pages are most and least popular and how visitors move around the site.

Information collected by these cookies is aggregated and anonymous. If you disable them, we may not be able to track usage patterns or optimise the website properly.

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we integrate into our pages.

If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of the related services may not function properly.

Targeting Cookies

These cookies may be set by our advertising partners to build a profile of your interests and show relevant adverts on other sites.

They do not store personal information directly but may identify your browser or device. If you disable them, you may receive less targeted advertising.

3. Third-Party Cookies

In some cases, we also use cookies provided by trusted third parties. The following section outlines the most common third-party cookies you may encounter on this site.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how you use the site and how we can improve the experience. These cookies may track time spent on pages and how users navigate between sections.

For more information on Google Analytics cookies and their usage, see the official Google Analytics documentation.

Social Media

We may also use social media buttons or plugins that allow you to connect with your social network. Those providers may set cookies through our site for the purposes described in their own privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies as you wish. You can remove cookies already stored on your device and configure most browsers to block them from being placed. If you do this, you may need to manually adjust certain preferences during future visits and some website features may stop working correctly.

Most web browsers allow control over cookies through the browser settings. For more information, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.
