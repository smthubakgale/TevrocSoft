Chatbot questions, answered

Can the chatbot answer anything?

It should work within agreed topics and approved information. When it cannot help confidently, the flow should say so and provide a clear route to a person or another next step.

Does WhatsApp require a particular setup?

Business account configuration, platform access, provider policies, and available features can affect implementation. These requirements are checked during planning rather than assumed.

Picture the customer journey

A friendly conversation can still have a clear structure.

The chatbot should make it easy to understand what it can help with and how to get a person when needed. A simple flow might welcome someone, identify their topic, provide the relevant approved information, and offer a next step.

04 · Continue

Make the next step clear
