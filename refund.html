Billing & Financial Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2023

1. Overview

At TevrocSoft, we provide transparent billing and financial policies to ensure clarity about project scope, delivery, and payment responsibilities.

This Refund Policy explains how refunds, returns, and exchanges are handled for purchases made through our website. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

2. Digital Products

Due to the nature of digital products, we generally do not offer refunds once a purchase is completed. However, we understand that exceptional circumstances can take place regarding the character of the product we supply.

Eligibility for Refund

We will honor refund requests under the following circumstances:

  • Non-delivery of the product: Due to some mailing issues, you might not receive a delivery email from us.
  • Major defects: Although all the products are thoroughly tested before release, unexpected errors may occur.
  • Product not-as-described: A request based on this reason is addressed on a case-by-case basis and subject to our approval.

Non-Refundable Situations

We do not grant refunds in the following situations:

  • You changed your mind about the product.
  • You bought a product by mistake.
  • You do not have sufficient expertise to use the product.
  • You can no longer access the product because it has been removed (we advise you to download items as soon as you have purchased them to avoid this situation).

3. Subscription Services

For subscription-based services, you may cancel your subscription at any time. However, we do not provide refunds for any partial subscription periods.

If you cancel your subscription, you will continue to have access to the service through the end of your current billing period.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our customer support at info@tevrocsoft.co.za with the following information:

  • Your full name
  • Order number
  • Product name
  • Reason for refund request
  • Any supporting documentation

We will process your request within 5-7 business days and notify you of the outcome via email.

5. Processing Refunds

If your refund request is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment. You will receive the credit within a certain amount of days, depending on your card issuer's policies.

Please note that processing times may vary depending on your financial institution. We will not be responsible for any delays caused by your bank or payment processor.

6. Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback or dispute with your bank or credit card company, we reserve the right to terminate all services immediately and permanently. We also reserve the right to dispute any chargeback that we believe to be fraudulent or unwarranted.

We strongly encourage you to contact us first to resolve any issues before initiating a chargeback.
