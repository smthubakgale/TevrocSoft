Billing & Financial Policy

Effective date: 01 September 2026 Last updated: 01 September 2026

This Billing and Financial Policy governs project fees, subscription charges, service deposits, and any request for refund or credit by clients of TevrocSoft. All financial arrangements are subject to the terms of the relevant proposal, invoice, or written agreement.

1. Overview

At TevrocSoft, we provide transparent billing and financial policies to ensure clarity about project scope, delivery, and payment responsibilities.

This policy explains how refunds, credit adjustments, and payment disputes are handled for purchases and services made through our website or agreed engagement channels.

2. Digital Products and Services

Due to the nature of digital products and custom project work, fees are generally non-refundable once service delivery has begun or when a digital product has been delivered to the client. However, TevrocSoft will consider exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis.

Eligibility for Refund or Credit

We may consider a refund or credit where:

  • The product or service was not delivered as agreed.
  • There was a material defect or failure in service delivery.
  • The service was misrepresented or not as described in the relevant agreement.
  • A significant billing error or duplicate charge occurred.

Non-Refundable Situations

We do not normally grant refunds in the following situations:

  • The client changed their mind after purchase or project work commenced.
  • The client purchased the service by mistake.
  • The client cannot effectively use the product or service due to lack of operational readiness or technical capability.
  • The service has been used, accessed, or consumed in line with the agreed terms.

3. Subscription Services

For subscription-based services, clients may cancel at any time in accordance with the agreement. However, fees for the current billing period are generally not refundable.

If a subscription is cancelled, access will continue through the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated in the signed agreement.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or credit, please contact customer support at info@tevrocsoft.co.za with the following information:

  • Your full name.
  • Order number or invoice reference.
  • Service or product name.
  • The reason for the request.
  • Any supporting documentation or evidence.

We will review the request and respond within the time period stated in the applicable agreement or as otherwise reasonably necessary.

5. Processing of Approved Requests

If a refund request is approved, we will initiate a credit to the original method of payment or provide another agreed resolution. Settlement times may vary depending on the financial institution or payment processor.

We are not responsible for delays caused by banking systems, card issuers, or third-party payment processors outside our direct control.

6. Chargebacks and Disputes

If a client files a chargeback or disputes a payment with a bank or card issuer, TevrocSoft reserves the right to suspend or terminate services immediately and to dispute any claim we believe to be unfounded or fraudulent.

We strongly encourage clients to contact us first to resolve concerns before escalating to a financial dispute process.
