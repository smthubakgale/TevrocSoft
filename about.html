About TevrocSoft

We build thoughtful software and digital experiences that help teams launch faster, serve customers better, and grow with confidence.

Our story

TevrocSoft started with a simple idea: bring strategy, design, and development together so businesses can turn ideas into reliable products without unnecessary friction.

Today we partner with founders, teams, and established operators to build custom web platforms, mobile apps, integrations, and digital growth systems.

Our team is led by Managing Director Thubakgale Mabalane Samuel, who guides strategy and delivery across every project.

“Our mission is to create practical digital tools that make everyday work clearer, faster, and more valuable.”

Part of South Africa's Digital Ecosystem

A company invested in the digital infrastructure behind local business

TevrocSoft is actively engaged in South Africa's evolving digital ecosystem, with our leadership participating in the .ZA Domain Name Authority (ZADNA) community.

Through this involvement, TevrocSoft stays connected to conversations around South Africa's internet infrastructure, domain-name ecosystem, digital policy and the continued development of a trusted online environment.

We believe that building South African technology isn't only about creating software — it's also about contributing to the digital ecosystem in which South African businesses, organisations and communities operate.
