From research to handoff

Give every design choice a job.

A polished interface is more than a collection of attractive screens. The layout, language, and interactions should help users understand what to do and feel confident doing it.

Learn the context

Clarify audiences, goals, constraints, and the tasks people need to complete.

Explore the structure

Organise content and sketch journeys before investing in detailed visual polish.

Test the direction

Use prototypes and feedback to find confusing steps and refine the experience.

Prepare for build

Document states, responsive behavior, and design details for implementation.

Clarity is part of accessibility

Readable contrast, understandable labels, predictable navigation, and layouts that adapt to different screens all help make an interface easier to use. The right accessibility considerations depend on the audience and product requirements.

Design and development work together

Considering technical constraints early helps the final experience remain practical to build and maintain. We can collaborate with your development team or shape a design handoff around the implementation plan.

Design questions, answered

Do we need research before a redesign?

The level of research depends on what is known and what is changing. Reviewing current journeys, feedback, and business goals can help avoid carrying existing problems into a new visual style.

What does a design handoff include?

It can include agreed screen designs, interaction states, responsive behavior, reusable visual guidance, and notes for developers. The exact deliverables are defined to suit the scope and build team.
