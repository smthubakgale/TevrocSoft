Infrastructure that fits

Reliable hosting begins with understanding your application.

The right setup depends on what you are hosting, how it is deployed, and who needs to maintain it. We help make those requirements visible so hosting choices are based on the product—not just a server size.

Plan the environment

Review the application stack, expected usage, storage needs, domains, and access requirements.

Prepare deployment

Coordinate configuration, DNS, SSL, and the steps needed to move a service into its hosting environment.

Maintain and review

Discuss updates, backups, monitoring, and support arrangements that match the agreed service scope.

Set expectations before a migration.

A move between hosting providers can involve DNS changes, application configuration, data transfer, and a planned transition. The exact steps depend on the current environment and access available, so we review dependencies and agree on responsibilities before proceeding.

Hosting questions, answered

Can you host any technology stack?

Hosting options depend on the application’s runtime, operating system, resource needs, and provider constraints. We review these requirements before recommending an environment.

What is included in ongoing support?

Support tasks and response expectations should be agreed as part of the service scope. Maintenance, monitoring, backups, and troubleshooting needs can differ from one product to another.

Good operational habits

Know what is running and how to recover.

Hosting is not just the initial deployment. A clear operational picture helps teams understand where an application lives, who can access it, and which recovery steps have been considered.

Access

Keep administrative access intentional and document who is responsible for important services.

Updates

Plan how operating systems, frameworks, and application dependencies will be reviewed and maintained.

Backups

Agree what needs to be backed up, how often, and how recovery is checked for your needs.

Visibility

Decide which service health or availability signals matter and who should review them.

Before choosing a VPS

Match the environment to the application.

A hosting recommendation should consider more than processor and memory. Runtime support, storage, network needs, operating system, deployment method, and the team’s operational experience can all affect the right fit.

Requirements may change as usage grows, so it is useful to understand which resources can be reviewed and adjusted later.

Shared responsibilities

Make ownership clear from day one.

Hosting involves the infrastructure provider, application owners, and whoever manages updates and access. A clear agreement helps everyone understand who handles account administration, changes, incidents, and recovery tasks.

The responsibilities and response arrangements are confirmed for each engagement; they are not assumed to be identical across all projects.

Plan for continuity

Know the steps to take when a service needs attention.

Every application has different operational needs. Agreeing how issues are reported, who can investigate, and how recovery is approached can reduce uncertainty when support is needed.

Notice

Decide which checks or service signals matter and who should be notified if they change.

Investigate

Keep relevant service details and access responsibilities clear for troubleshooting.

Recover

Document agreed recovery options and verify that backups match your recovery needs.
