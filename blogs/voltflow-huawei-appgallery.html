VoltFlow is now on Huawei AppGallery.

VoltFlow's electronics design workspace is now available to Huawei users through AppGallery, bringing circuit design and connected-device project tools to another mobile audience.

October 2, 2026 · 3 min read

A new way to get VoltFlow on Huawei devices

VoltFlow brings the early stages of electronics projects into one workspace. Makers can arrange components, work with Arduino C or MicroPython, simulate circuit behavior, and keep schematics and PCB planning close to the original idea.

Publishing on Huawei AppGallery makes VoltFlow easier to discover and install for Huawei users. It extends the product's reach while keeping the same focus: make a connected-device idea easier to build, inspect, and share.

VoltFlow supports projects involving popular microcontrollers, sensors, displays, and connectivity workflows. Whether exploring a first circuit or reviewing an IoT prototype, users can work through the design with the project context in view.
