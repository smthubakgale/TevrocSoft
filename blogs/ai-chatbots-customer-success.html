Customer support doesn't stop at 5 PM. With AI chatbots, your website can provide instant answers, qualify leads, and resolve common issues 24/7—without hiring a large support team. That's why we've made chatbot development a core part of what TevrocSoft offers.

The Challenge: Always-On Support

Growing businesses face a common problem: customer inquiries come in all hours, but your support team is limited. People get frustrated waiting for responses. Leads go cold. Questions pile up.

"We get hundreds of messages each week," one of our clients told us. "Half are the same questions. We're drowning in support tickets."

Enter: Intelligent Chatbots

A smart chatbot can handle the repetitive work—answering FAQs, collecting customer information, scheduling consultations, even qualifying sales leads. When a conversation needs human attention, it hands off seamlessly to your team.

What AI Chatbots Can Do

  • ✓
    Answer FAQs Instantly
    Customers get immediate responses to common questions—pricing, features, support, etc. No waiting.
  • ✓
    Qualify Leads
    Ask qualifying questions to identify hot prospects and route them to your sales team faster.
  • ✓
    Schedule Meetings
    Automate booking consultations directly from the chat, no back-and-forth emails needed.
  • ✓
    Collect Information
    Gather contact details, project requirements, and preferences without forms.
  • ✓
    Escalate Intelligently
    Route complex issues to humans with full context already gathered.
