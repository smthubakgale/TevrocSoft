What ClientFlow solves

Many businesses still juggle customer queries, orders, invoices, appointments, stock levels, and team communication across email, spreadsheets, and phone calls. ClientFlow brings these pieces together into a single web application so teams can keep every client conversation, order update and service booking in one place.

The platform is built around core operations: customer and team management, order and inventory control, invoicing and accounting workflows, delivery logistics, and performance reporting. That means the same system can support sales, service delivery, stock oversight and internal coordination.

Key goals

  • Unify client profiles, orders, appointments, inventory and delivery checklists.
  • Replace manual spreadsheets, notes and WhatsApp coordination.
  • Give teams better visibility across order status, stock levels, billing and follow-up.
  • Keep the experience mobile-friendly for field teams and office managers alike.

Business impact

  • Reduce missed appointments and order delays with smarter scheduling and tracking.
  • Keep clients informed with clear status updates and delivery notifications.
  • Cut back on time spent in email, calls and manual accounting reconciliations.
  • Make day-to-day operations easier for small teams by centralizing team tasks, inventory and financial workflows.

A polished app experience for clients and teams

Public and private workflows

The public side of ClientFlow is designed to engage clients with simple booking and status lookup pages. The private side gives staff a secure workspace for managing clients, approvals, invoices, and delivery progress.

Public pages

  • Service request form with clear fields.
  • Delivery tracking and appointment confirmations.
  • Support options with quick client contact.
  • SEO-friendly pages for discovery.

Private pages

  • Secure login, registration, and password reset.
  • Client management with order history, invoices and outstanding balances.
  • Appointment scheduling, task assignment, inventory control and supplier tracking.
  • Team coordination, payroll-ready accounting tools and reporting views.

Built with speed and flexibility

ClientFlow uses React and Vite for fast loading and smooth interaction. Shared components, clean navigation, and SEO-conscious public pages make the platform easy to evolve and maintain.
